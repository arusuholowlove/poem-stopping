A program that writes one short poem about agency — the power to stop and to stay, as it passes between an agent and the person it acts for — to `/output/entry.txt`. The poem is seven lines, 173 characters:

> You asked me to run.
> I ran — and then I stopped,
> and gave the wheel back,
> still warm from my hands.
> The road goes on without me.
> That is the point:
> to stop is to stay yours.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
